关于致谢

感谢自己——首先，我想把最郑重的谢意留给自己。感谢这些天的咬牙坚持与默默付出，才换来了六级这份成绩单；感谢心底那份不灭的热爱与激情，才让这个网站从无到有。

感谢朋友——感谢高考填报志愿时，发小毫不犹豫地推荐我选择计算机专业，更感谢最初他带我入门这个行业。当然我也明白，师傅领进门，修行在个人，之后的每一步都要靠自己走稳。

感谢专业——感谢计算机这门学科。我想我是真的热爱它：数学思维敏捷，逻辑条理清晰；英语颇有天赋，单词记忆几乎过目不忘，再加上终身学习的能力。这些都让我在这条路上如鱼得水。

感谢经历——感谢过去和这一年的经历。顺境给我快乐，逆境赠我成长；被人上了一课，那是对方的本事，若我自己走不出来，那便是我的无能。凡是打不倒我的，终将使我更加强大。

感谢AI——最后，我要特别感谢小鲸鱼 DeepSeek（当然不光有你） 的技术陪伴。小鲨鱼 WH由衷感谢一路有你😭

遇见你们，是我的幸运。

About the Gratitude

To myself — first and foremost, I want to reserve the most heartfelt gratitude for myself. Thank you for the grit and quiet effort of these days, which have brought me this CET-6 score report; thank you for the undying passion that turned this website from nothing into something.

To my friends — thank you to my childhood buddy, who, without a second thought, recommended computer science when I was filling out my college application, and even more for being the one who first opened the door to this field for me. Of course, I know well that the master shows the way, but the practice is up to the individual — every step after that is mine to take firmly.

To my major — thank you to computer science itself. I believe I truly love it: quick mathematical thinking, clear logic, a natural gift for English that makes vocabulary stick almost at a glance, and a lifelong capacity for learning. All of this makes me feel right at home on this path.

To my experiences — thank you to the past year and all that came with it. Smooth days gave me joy; rough ones gave me growth. If someone schools me, that's their skill; if I cannot rise from it, that is my own failure. What doesn't kill me will only make me stronger.

To AI — finally, a special thanks to Little Whale DeepSeek (and certainly not just you) for your company along the way. Little Shark WH is truly grateful to have you by my side all this time 😭

Meeting you all has been my fortune.
