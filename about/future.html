关于未来

网站嘛，有了第一个，未来便会有无数个。如你所见，便是我做的第一个网站。既是处女之作，便赋予了我特别的意义，毕竟是从零到一的突破。也正因此，难免透着新手的稚嫩。

未来，此站，我会偶尔回来更新一下，也算回头看看自己的初心与起点。当然，才大一下，来日方长。所以未来，我还会不定期创作新的作品。每次完工，我都会第一时间，与大家分享创作的喜悦，以及创作中遇到的琐碎轶事与奇思妙想。

未来，不仅是对网站，更是对自己说的。未来的你，很优秀，他会站在更高的地方，感谢现在拼命努力，只为不留遗憾的自己…

📌 目前此站，只是一个纯前端网站，考虑未来加入后端技术栈。
— Translated by English —

About the Future

Websites—once you have the first, countless more will follow. This one here is my very first. As a debut work, it carries a meaning all its own—a leap from nothing to something. And for that very reason, it wears its beginner's rawness without apology.

In the days ahead, I may drop by this little corner from time to time, to brush it up and to remind myself of where it all began. And yes, I'm only a freshman in my second semester, plenty of time lies ahead. So I'll keep crafting new projects now and then. Each time one is finished, I'll come straight here to share the joy of making it, along with the little stories and odd ideas that sparked along the way.

The future, though, is not just about websites—it's a word I say to myself. The future you—bright and capable—will stand on higher ground, looking back with gratitude at the present self who gives everything, so that when that day comes, there will be no room for regret…

📌 Currently, this site is purely front-end; I am considering adding back-end technologies in the future.
