时间给的答案

文 / 吴横 ｜ 发布于 2026.08.24

普通双非一本六级600+，六级备考路亦是人生之路。

2026年6月29日，我特地重温了一遍KPL的传世神作——AG纪录片《时间给的答案》。

2026年6月30日，偶然刷到了一条抖音，一个关于小女孩六级备考失败受挫却鼓起勇气发出来的纪录片。

我想，和小女孩六级备考一样，无论成功还是失败。我熬过的这一年的"人生路"和两个月的"六级备考路"同样不该被埋没！

我想，和AG这条纪录片一样，时间会给出我"人生路"和"六级备考路"上的答案！

2026年7月1日，我回想起过去和这一年的人生经历，在路上走着走着就哭了。

时间过的真快啊，转眼间，我流放在外省上大学，已经快一年了。

摸爬滚打着，那个从小到大靠家人和朋友照顾的小屁孩，他自己一个人，走过来了。

我想，人生必须有一段经历，是自己走过去的，它充满了痛苦，充满了孤独。但那便是成长——

他长大懂事了，找到自我了。

他长大懂事了——开始独当一面了起来。做到了，在生活上——不求他人，只靠自己。

"靠自己的时候，你就会知道，有谁、没谁、多谁、少谁，真的一点都不重要"。

他找到自我了——活得清醒，活得透彻。无需他人理解，不与他人争辩。

"你所见即是我，好与坏我都不反驳"。

或许，时间给了我人生路上的答案！

感我此言良久立，再谈谈我的六级备考之路吧！

两个月的六级备考。

日复一日的六级单词背诵，为期近一个月的刷真题套卷并订正，还有一次次的听力模拟。学习很苦，但坚持很酷。

这两个月内，我几乎切断了所有人际交往，主动选择了独处。在独处中沉淀自己，在学习中充实自己。

"内心充盈者，独行也如众"。

或许，时间给了我六级路上的答案！

2026年6月13日，六级考场上的我既紧张又从容，但却发挥得异常顺利。

两个月的出分等待。

2026年8月24日，六级查分——高考失利、普通双非一本的我，很幸运，大一下第一次六级600+。

或是运气，又或是努力，亦或是实力——这次六级，最难且我最薄弱的听力，竟然A篇和C篇全对，只错了B篇三条！写译成绩超我所愿。而简单点的阅读却答得中规中矩。

六级600+的成功，来之不易。但我知道，这就是我应得的。

我太需要这次成功来证明——

给自己看——你一直很优秀！那年那次，你只是输给了自己，而不是输了人生。你的人生答卷，乱答也是满分。

给别人看——在这里，我就是有"心高气傲"的实力和底气。

少年心气是不可再生之物——我不安于现状，更不甘于平庸。

这次六级，只是我人生路上的起点。

因为，再等等，时间给的答案，常看常新……

— Translated by English —

What Time Answers

A mere undergraduate from an ordinary university, yet CET-6 score above 600. The road to CET-6 is also the road of my life.

On June 29, 2026, I revisited a legendary KPL documentary — AG's What Time Answers.

On June 30, 2026, I stumbled upon a video of a young girl bravely sharing her failure in the CET-6 exam.

I thought to myself: like that girl, whether in success or failure, the year I've endured and the two months I've devoted to this exam should not go unnoticed.

And like AG's documentary, time will eventually give me its answer — on both roads.

On July 1, 2026, as I walked down the street, looking back on the past year, I broke down in tears.

How time flies. In the blink of an eye, I've been away from home, studying in another province, for nearly a year.

Stumbling and crawling, that little kid who had always relied on family and friends — he made it through. Alone.

I suppose there must be a stretch of life that one walks entirely by oneself — filled with pain, filled with solitude. But that — that is growth.

He has grown up, come to understand, and found himself.

He has learned to stand on his own — in life, relying on no one but himself.

"When you rely on yourself, you come to realize that who's there, who's not, who's more, who's less — none of it truly matters."

He lives with clarity, with transparency — seeking no one's understanding, arguing with no one.

"What you see is what I am — I won't refute either the good or the bad."

Perhaps time has already given me its answer on the road of life.

With these words lingering in my heart, let me turn back to my CET-6 journey.

Two months of preparation — daily vocabulary drills, nearly a month of past papers and corrections, and countless listening exercises. The grind was tough, but persistence is a kind of beauty.

During those two months, I cut off almost all social contact, choosing solitude by will. In solitude, I found my center; in study, I found my fullness.

"A heart full of abundance walks as boldly alone as in a crowd."

Perhaps time has given me its answer on the road to CET-6 as well.

On June 13, 2026, I sat for the exam — nervous yet composed, and it went far smoother than I had dared to hope.

Then came the two-month wait.

On August 24, 2026, the results were out — and I, a student from an ordinary university, having stumbled in the college entrance exam, scored over 600 on my very first attempt in the second semester of freshman year.

Perhaps it was luck, or hard work, or simply capability — but in this exam, my weakest and most feared section, listening, saw me get all correct in Parts A and C, missing only three in Part B; my writing and translation exceeded expectations, while the reading section, supposedly the easier part, was merely average.

The success was hard-earned, but I know — I deserved it.

I needed this triumph to prove — to myself: I have always been capable. That failure back then was a loss to myself, not to life. Even a scribbled answer sheet to life's questions can still be perfect. To others: Here, I carry the confidence and the strength to hold my head high.

The spirit of youth is a non-renewable resource — I am not content with where I am, nor resigned to mediocrity.

This CET-6 victory is but a starting point on my life's journey.

Because — wait a little longer. The answer given by time reveals new meanings with each passing glance…
